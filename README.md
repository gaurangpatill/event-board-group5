This PR addresses the technical debt and integration inconsistencies identified during the conclusion of Sprint 1. The primary goal is to stabilize the dev branch by resolving architectural mismatches and fixing specific UI/state bugs.

Retrospective: Sprint 1 Bottlenecks

    - Integration Delay: A significant portion of the codebase was merged on the final day of the sprint. This led to a bottleneck, preventing thorough peer review and early detection of conflicts.

    - Lack of Contract: Development began individually without a fully finalized "contract" or shared interface definition. This resulted in integration friction when merging different people's features.

    - External Time Constraints: Overlapping academic deadlines and exams limited the team’s ability to communicate and do work in sync, leading to a reliance on independent, isolated, and asynchronous workflows. This caused significant, chaotic, issues upon attempting to merge together our changes through pull requests.

Prospective Changes:

    - Continuous Integration: PRs are now being pushed and reviewed incrementally throughout the sprint cycle rather than at the deadline.

    - United Decision-Making: Important, structural changes are run by the group first rather than trying to adjust everyone's code after-the-fact.

    - Proactive Conflict Management: We have established more frequent, brief check-ins to identify blockers earlier, making sure to consistently update each other on what is being worked on.

    - Active Communication: We established a better way to commuinicate when people are busy and timelines. Allowing for the group to make adjustments on conflicts in case academic deadlines get in the way in the future.
